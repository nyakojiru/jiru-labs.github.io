epub2audiobook

Python · MIT

Turns an epub into a chaptered audiobook. Gemini TTS does the narration; voice conversion against a single reference clip then anchors the timbre, so the voice stops changing every time the model starts a new generation. It ships with the measured research behind it: engine defects, blind listening rounds, and the fixes that improved the numbers while sounding worse.

Data analysis — Madrid residential market

We track the residential supply of Madrid and its metropolitan area daily, listing by listing, from the day it is published until it disappears. That series yields figures an average-price report cannot give: how long each zone actually takes to sell, what share of sellers end up cutting the price, by how much, and how long they wait before doing it.

Time on market is estimated with survival analysis: listings still active are not discarded, they inform the estimate up to the last observed date. That is what avoids the optimistic bias of averaging only what has already sold. Aggregates are cross-checked against the INE's official transaction statistics for the province.

Estimates that do not reach sufficient resolution are published as such —“> 88 days” rather than an invented median— and every figure travels with its margin. A number that cannot carry a decision is worse than no number.

Delivered as a PDF report with the per-zone analysis, plus the working data in Excel. A one-off study over the accumulated history, or a quarterly update.
